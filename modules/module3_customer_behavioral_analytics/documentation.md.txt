
# Module 3: Customer Intelligence & Behavioral Analytics
---

# 1. Objective

This module focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior to extract strategic insights about customer value, retention patterns, purchasing frequency, and behavioral segmentation.

The objective is to transform transactional customer data into actionable intelligence that supports customer strategy, revenue optimization, and long-term value assessment.

The analysis generates behavioral insights across:

* Customer Lifetime Value
* Purchase Frequency
* Customer Retention
* Repeat Purchase Behavior
* Customer Segmentation
* Customer Revenue Contribution

---

# 2. Dataset Overview

**Source Table:** `superstore_clean`

### Key Columns Used

* `customer_id`
* `order_id`
* `order_date`
* `sales`
* `profit`
* `segment`
* `region`

The dataset contains customer-level transaction records used to evaluate purchasing behavior, customer value, and long-term engagement patterns.

---

# 3. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Analysis (Completed)

## 3.1 Objective

Estimate the long-term revenue value generated by each customer using aggregated transactional data.

This analysis identifies high-value customers and quantifies their total contribution to company revenue.

---

## 3.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Revenue per Customer
* Total Profit per Customer
* Total Orders per Customer
* Average Order Value per Customer
* Estimated Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

---

## 3.3 Analytical Purpose

CLV analysis enables:

* Identification of high-value customers
* Customer revenue concentration analysis
* Customer profitability benchmarking
* Strategic prioritization of customer segments

---

## 3.4 Techniques Applied

* Customer-Level Aggregation (`GROUP BY customer_id`)
* Order-Based Revenue Summation
* Profit Aggregation
* Average Order Value Computation
* Multi-Metric Customer Ranking

This phase establishes the foundational **customer value intelligence layer**.

---

# 4. Customer Retention & Repeat Purchase Analysis (Completed)

## 4.1 Objective

Evaluate customer loyalty by measuring repeat purchasing behavior and identifying retention patterns.

This analysis distinguishes between one-time buyers and recurring customers.

---

## 4.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Orders per Customer
* Repeat Purchase Indicator
* Unique Customers vs Returning Customers
* Customer Purchase Frequency
* Retention Rate Estimation

---

## 4.3 Analytical Purpose

Retention analysis enables:

* Identification of loyal customer segments
* Detection of one-time purchase behavior
* Understanding of purchase frequency distribution
* Evaluation of customer engagement stability

---

## 4.4 Techniques Applied

* Order Count Aggregation
* Conditional Customer Classification
* Purchase Frequency Analysis
* Repeat Purchase Detection
* Customer-Level Behavioral Labeling

This phase establishes the **customer retention intelligence layer**.

---

# 5. RFM Customer Segmentation (Completed)

## 5.1 Objective

Develop behavioral customer segmentation using the **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) framework**.

RFM segmentation categorizes customers based on how recently they purchased, how often they purchase, and how much revenue they generate.

---

## 5.2 RFM Metrics Computed

* **Recency** – Days since last purchase
* **Frequency** – Total number of orders
* **Monetary Value** – Total revenue generated by customer

---

## 5.3 Segmentation Framework

Customers are classified into behavioral segments such as:

* High-Value Customers
* Loyal Customers
* Potential Loyalists
* At-Risk Customers
* Dormant Customers

---

## 5.4 Analytical Purpose

RFM segmentation enables:

* Identification of high-value customer cohorts
* Detection of declining customer engagement
* Prioritization of retention strategies
* Targeted marketing segmentation
* Behavioral customer profiling

---

## 5.5 Techniques Applied

* Window Functions for RFM Scoring
* Ranking-Based Segmentation
* Customer-Level Aggregation
* Conditional Segment Classification
* Multi-Metric Behavioral Modeling

This phase establishes the **customer behavioral intelligence layer**.

---

# 7. Business Observations (Current)

* Customer revenue contribution is highly concentrated among a smaller subset of customers.
* Repeat purchasing behavior significantly influences total revenue generation.
* High-value customers generate disproportionate revenue compared to average customers.
* Customer engagement varies significantly across purchasing frequency levels.
