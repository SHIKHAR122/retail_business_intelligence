# Module 5: Time Series Trend Analysis

---

## 1. Objective

This module focuses on analyzing **temporal trends in revenue and sales** to identify seasonal patterns, month-over-month and year-over-year growth, cumulative revenue trends, and anomalies.

The goal is to transform transactional time-series data into actionable intelligence that supports **forecasting, trend identification, and strategic business planning**.

---

## 2. Dataset Overview

**Source Table:** `superstore_clean`

### Key Columns Used

* `order_date`
* `sales`
* `profit`
* `product_name`
* `category`
* `sub_category`

The dataset contains **transaction-level sales and profit data with timestamps**, suitable for time-based aggregations and trend analysis.

---

## 3. Monthly Revenue Trend

### 3.1 Objective

Analyze revenue patterns for each month to identify high and low performing months.

### 3.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Revenue per Month
* Month-wise Revenue Ranking

### 3.3 Steps

1. Aggregate revenue grouped by month
2. Order results chronologically
3. Visualize month-over-month patterns

### 3.4 Analytical Purpose

Reveals **revenue fluctuations across months**, supporting **seasonal planning and operational forecasting**.

---

## 4. Monthly Profit Trend

### 4.1 Objective

Assess monthly profit to understand profitability trends over time.

### 4.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Profit per Month
* Profit Contribution Relative to Revenue

### 4.3 Steps

1. Aggregate profit by month
2. Compare with monthly revenue
3. Identify profitable or loss-making months

### 4.4 Analytical Purpose

Shows **profitability trends**, highlighting periods of high or low margin performance.

---

## 5. Seasonal Sales Patterns

### 5.1 Objective

Identify recurring **seasonal patterns in sales**, such as spikes during holidays or specific quarters.

### 5.2 Metrics Computed

* Average Revenue per Month across Years
* Deviation from Mean Revenue

### 5.3 Steps

1. Aggregate monthly revenue for multiple years
2. Compute average and standard deviation per month
3. Identify months with consistent peaks or troughs

### 5.4 Analytical Purpose

Supports **seasonal inventory planning, marketing campaigns, and resource allocation**.

---

## 6. Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth

### 6.1 Objective

Measure **annual growth in revenue and profit** to track long-term business expansion.

### 6.2 Metrics Computed

* Revenue and Profit for Current Year vs Previous Year
* YoY Growth %
  **Formula:** `(Current Year Revenue - Previous Year Revenue) ÷ Previous Year Revenue × 100`

### 6.3 Steps

1. Aggregate yearly revenue and profit
2. Compare each year with the previous
3. Calculate YoY growth percentages

### 6.4 Analytical Purpose

Highlights **long-term growth trends**, revealing **years of significant performance improvement or decline**.

---

## 7. Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth

### 7.1 Objective

Measure **short-term growth trends** by comparing consecutive months.

### 7.2 Metrics Computed

* Monthly Revenue
* MoM Growth %
  **Formula:** `(Current Month Revenue - Previous Month Revenue) ÷ Previous Month Revenue × 100`

### 7.3 Steps

1. Aggregate revenue by month
2. Sort months chronologically
3. Compare each month with the previous to compute growth

### 7.4 Analytical Purpose

Reveals **short-term fluctuations in performance**, useful for monitoring **promotions, campaigns, and operational impact**.

---

## 8. Rolling Revenue Trends (Moving Average)

### 8.1 Objective

Smooth **monthly revenue fluctuations** to identify the underlying trend.

### 8.2 Metrics Computed

* 3-Month (or 6-Month) Moving Average of Revenue

### 8.3 Steps

1. Compute monthly revenue
2. Apply a rolling window average (e.g., 3 months)
3. Analyze smoothed trend to detect **stable growth or decline patterns**

### 8.4 Analytical Purpose

Reduces the noise from short-term fluctuations and highlights **sustained revenue trends**, improving forecasting and operational planning.

---

## 9. Revenue Growth Over Time (Cumulative Revenue)

### 9.1 Objective

Track **progressive revenue accumulation**, reset annually to observe yearly growth.

### 9.2 Metrics Computed

* Cumulative Revenue per Year
  **Formula:** `SUM(Monthly Revenue) OVER (PARTITION BY Year ORDER BY Month ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW)`

### 9.3 Steps

1. Aggregate monthly revenue
2. Partition by year
3. Apply cumulative sum to track revenue accumulation

### 9.4 Analytical Purpose

Shows **how revenue builds within a year**, identifying periods of accelerated or slowed growth and supporting annual performance assessment.

---

## 10. Revenue Contribution by Year

### 10.1 Objective

Determine the **proportion of total revenue generated by each year**.

### 10.2 Metrics Computed

* Revenue per Year
* Revenue Contribution %
  **Formula:** `Revenue per Year ÷ Total Revenue × 100`

### 10.3 Steps

1. Aggregate revenue by year
2. Compute total revenue across all years
3. Calculate each year’s percentage contribution

### 10.4 Analytical Purpose

Highlights **years that contributed most to total business revenue**, supporting long-term strategic analysis.

---

## 11. Sales Volatility / Anomaly Detection

### 11.1 Objective

Identify **months with abnormal revenue** that deviate significantly from expected trends.

### 11.2 Metrics Computed

* Monthly Revenue
* Average Monthly Revenue
* Sales Pattern Classification (`CASE` logic):

  * Spike: Revenue > 130% of average
  * Drop: Revenue < 70% of average
  * Normal: Otherwise

### 11.3 Steps

1. Compute monthly revenue
2. Calculate overall average monthly revenue
3. Classify each month using `CASE` logic
4. Flag anomalies

### 11.4 Analytical Purpose

Detects **unusual spikes or drops in sales**, enabling investigation of **promotions, market changes, or operational issues**.

---

## 12. Analytical Techniques Applied

* **GROUP BY Aggregations** (Monthly, Yearly)
* **Window Functions** (`SUM` for cumulative revenue, `AVG` for moving average)
* **Conditional Logic (`CASE`)** for anomaly detection
* **Ratio Calculations** (Revenue Contribution %, Growth %)
* **Cumulative Sum Computation** (Revenue Growth Over Time)
* **Rolling Average Computation** (Moving Average)
* Chronological Sorting for time-based trend analysis

---

## 13. Business Insights Developed

* Revenue and profit show **clear seasonal patterns**, helping plan inventory and promotions.
* MoM and YoY growth metrics reveal **short-term fluctuations vs long-term trends**.
* Rolling averages highlight **sustained growth trends** while reducing volatility noise.
* Cumulative revenue within a year shows **how revenue accumulates and which months drive growth**.
* Yearly revenue contribution identifies **high-performing years** in the business lifecycle.
* Anomaly detection identifies **unexpected spikes or drops**, signaling **market, operational, or promotional impacts**.
