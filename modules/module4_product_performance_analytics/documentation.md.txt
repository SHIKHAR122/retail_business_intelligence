# Module 4: Product Performance Intelligence

---

## 1. Objective

This module focuses on evaluating product-level performance to identify high-value products, underperforming products, and category-level contributions to business growth and profitability.

The goal is to transform transactional product data into actionable intelligence that supports inventory optimization, revenue maximization, and strategic decision-making.

---

## 2. Dataset Overview

**Source Table:** `superstore_clean`

### Key Columns Used

* `product_name`
* `category`
* `sub_category`
* `sales`
* `profit`
* `quantity`
* `order_id`
* `order_date`

The dataset contains product-level transaction records used to analyze revenue, profit, sales volume, and product efficiency.

---

## 3. Best Selling Products

### 3.1 Objective

Identify products generating the highest revenue.

### 3.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Revenue per Product
* Total Quantity Sold
* Product Revenue Ranking

### 3.3 Steps

1. Group dataset by `product_name`
2. Aggregate `Total Revenue` and `Total Quantity Sold`
3. Rank products by revenue (descending)
4. Identify Top N Products

### 3.4 Analytical Purpose

Reveals which products drive the majority of sales and provides context for inventory and marketing focus.

---

## 4. Most Profitable Products

### 4.1 Objective

Identify products generating maximum profit, highlighting true business drivers.

### 4.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Profit per Product
* Total Revenue per Product
* Product Profit Ranking

### 4.3 Steps

1. Group dataset by `product_name`
2. Aggregate `Total Profit` and `Total Revenue`
3. Rank products by profit (descending)

### 4.4 Analytical Purpose

Shows products that contribute most to profitability, not just revenue, helping separate volume-driven vs value-driven products.

---

## 5. Low Performing Products

### 5.1 Objective

Detect products with low sales **and** low profit.

### 5.2 Metrics Computed

* Revenue per Product
* Profit per Product
* Quantity Sold per Product

### 5.3 Steps

1. Group dataset by `product_name`
2. Aggregate revenue, profit, and quantity
3. Identify bottom percentile products

### 5.4 Analytical Purpose

Highlights candidates for:

* Inventory reduction
* Discount removal
* Product discontinuation

---

## 6. Negative Margin Products

### 6.1 Objective

Detect products where total profit is negative.

### 6.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Profit per Product
* Negative Profit Filter

### 6.3 Steps

1. Group dataset by `product_name`
2. Aggregate total profit
3. Filter products where profit < 0

### 6.4 Analytical Purpose

Reveals products that destroy business value. Signals analytical maturity in interviews.

---

## 7. Category Contribution to Revenue

### 7.1 Objective

Determine how much each category contributes to total sales.

### 7.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Revenue per Category
* Revenue Contribution Percentage

### 7.3 Steps

1. Aggregate revenue by category
2. Calculate percentage contribution relative to total business revenue

### 7.4 Analytical Purpose

Shows which categories drive the business and aids strategic prioritization.

---

## 8. Profit Contribution by Category

### 8.1 Objective

Identify which categories contribute actual profit, not just revenue.

### 8.2 Metrics Computed

* Total Profit per Category
* Profit Contribution Percentage

### 8.3 Steps

1. Aggregate profit by category
2. Compute percentage contribution

### 8.4 Analytical Purpose

Differentiates between high-volume and high-profit categories for strategic insights.

---

## 9. Product Profit Margin Analysis

### 9.1 Objective

Evaluate profitability efficiency of each product.

### 9.2 Metrics Computed

* Profit Margin per Product
  **Formula:** Profit Margin = Total Profit ÷ Total Revenue

### 9.3 Steps

1. Group dataset by `product_name`
2. Compute revenue and profit
3. Calculate profit margin
4. Rank products by margin

### 9.4 Analytical Purpose

* High margin products = premium products
* Low margin products = volume-driven products
* Supports product pricing, discounting, and prioritization decisions

---

## 10. Pareto Analysis (80/20 Rule)

### 10.1 Objective

Determine whether 20% of products generate 80% of revenue.

### 10.2 Metrics Computed

* Cumulative Revenue Contribution per Product

### 10.3 Steps

1. Rank products by revenue (descending)
2. Calculate cumulative revenue contribution
3. Identify products contributing to 80% of total revenue

### 10.4 Analytical Purpose

Highlights business dependency on a small set of products and informs inventory, marketing, and sales focus.

---

## 11. Analytical Techniques Applied

* GROUP BY Aggregations
* Window Functions (`DENSE_RANK`, `RANK`)
* Conditional Logic (`CASE`)
* Ratio Calculations (Revenue Contribution %, Profit Margin %)
* Cumulative Sum Computation (Pareto Analysis)
* Multi-Metric Product Ranking
* Comparative Benchmarking

---

## 12. Business Insights Developed

* Best-selling products do not always produce the highest profit.
* Certain products generate losses despite revenue.
* Product efficiency differs across categories.
* Revenue contribution varies by category, separating volume vs value drivers.
* Pareto principle often applies: few products drive majority of revenue.
* Low-performing and negative-margin products can be targeted for operational optimization.
